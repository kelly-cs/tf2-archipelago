# Representative full-catalog nonpass controls

These are separate fresh-map diagnostics. They do not replace any first-pass
50× results or count toward the full sweep's score. One isolated two-CPU,
3 GiB worker used the same complete community image, plugins, seed and enabled
robot limiter. Only the requested clock speed varied.

Akure's Diamond and Dross wave 1 passed at 20×, 50× and again at 20×, with
substantially different durations and spawn counts. Its full-sweep timeout
was not reproduced simply by selecting 50× on a fresh map. Area 52's
Extraterrestrial Epidemic wave 1 also timed out at 20×, so that observed stall
is not unique to 50×. Neither result establishes a production-game root cause;
mission state, probe strategy and load remain possible factors. Raw results
and nonpass snapshots are preserved here and in the original full sweep.
