# Archipelago words, for MvM players

Archipelago is a multiworld randomizer. Several players, each in their own
game, share one pool of items. When you reach a place in your game, you find an
item. Often it belongs to somebody else. When they reach a place in theirs,
they find one of yours.

Archipelago's own [glossary](https://archipelago.gg/glossary) and
[FAQ](https://archipelago.gg/faq) explain the general terms. This page covers
what they mean here.

## The words

| Word | In Archipelago | Here, in Mann vs Machine |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Multiworld** | One session shared by every player | The room your server joins |
| **Slot** | One player's game inside the multiworld | Your whole server. One slot, however many people play on it. |
| **Seed** | The file that describes one multiworld | What the Archipelago app generates from your options |
| **Room** | A hosted seed, with an address | What you upload the seed to at `archipelago.gg` |
| **Check** | A place you reach in your game | A wave you clear, a mission you clear, the first tank or giant of a mission |
| **Item** | Something you receive | A class, a weapon slot, a mission ticket, a weapon buff, a cash bundle, a trap |
| **Goal** | What ends your part of the multiworld | The hardest mission of the run, or a share of the missions |
| **Hint** | Asking where an item is | Typed in the game chat with `!ap hint` |

Two words collide with the game's own vocabulary:

- **Slot.** Archipelago's slot is your server. Mann vs Machine's slot is a weapon
  slot: primary, secondary, melee. When this book says "weapon slot", it means
  the weapon.
- **Item.** Archipelago's item is something the run gives you for good. Mann vs
  Machine's item is a pickup that vanishes at the end of the wave. This book
  says "item" in the Archipelago sense.

## Two rules that surprise Archipelago players

- **The server shares every unlock.** A class the run finds opens for everybody
  on the server, at the same moment. There is no unlock per player.
- **A check belongs to the team.** The team clears the wave together, and that
  is the check. Who was on the server does not matter.

## Two rules that surprise MvM players

- **You start with almost nothing.** One to four classes and one to three weapon
  slots, depending on the difficulty of the run. You find the rest.
- **A locked weapon slot is empty.** The server removes the weapon at every
  spawn and every resupply until the run finds that slot.
