# Archipelago for MvM players

Archipelago's own [glossary](https://archipelago.gg/glossary) and
[FAQ](https://archipelago.gg/faq) explain multiworld, slot, seed, item, check
and filler. They apply here unchanged, so this page does not repeat them.

What follows is the part that is ours: the words Archipelago and Mann vs Machine
both use, and mean differently.

| Word | In Archipelago | In Mann vs Machine |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Check | A location you send when you reach it | Nothing. The game has no such idea. |
| Item | Something another world sends you | Something you pick up in a wave, and lose at the end of it |
| Slot | One player's game in the multiworld | A weapon slot: primary, secondary, melee |
| Mission | Not a term | An ordered series of waves, on one map |

A "slot unlock" here is an Archipelago **item** that opens a Mann vs Machine
**weapon slot**. Both senses of the word sit in that one sentence.
[`CONTEXT.md`](../../CONTEXT.md) settles every other collision.

Two rules of this world surprise people who know Archipelago:

- **The server shares every unlock.** One slot fills for everybody on it, not
  for the player who sent the check. One Archipelago slot covers the server, not
  one per player.
- **A check belongs to the team, not to you.** Your team clears the wave
  together, and everybody on the server at that moment makes the check.

[What the randomizer changes](./what-the-randomizer-changes.md) says what that
does to a game of MvM.
