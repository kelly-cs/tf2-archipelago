Anton, Source Sans 3 and IBM Plex Mono are here under the SIL Open Font
License 1.1, which allows redistribution inside a bundled application.

  Anton          https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Anton
  Source Sans 3  https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Source+Sans+3
  IBM Plex Mono  https://fonts.google.com/specimen/IBM+Plex+Mono

Only the latin subset of each is here. The files ship with the launcher rather
than being fetched from Google: the launcher runs on loopback for one player,
who may be offline, and a font request would be the only thing in it that left
the machine.
